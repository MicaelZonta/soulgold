# Ariana — página 2 (meio)

**Data no topo:** "The first crescent." (a primeira lua em um ano) · **Tinta:** a letra firme, e a segunda, menor, na margem

> The first sliver of moon in a year. The whole town went out to look at it.
>
> She went north instead, to a lake no map in Mahogany shows. Still water, a thin moon, people asleep along the shore. All of them smiling.
>
> She lay down among them. She had her first dream in years: a quiet lake, and fish that were only fish.
>
> (In the margin, smaller: “Not her dream. The vibrations are too slow for a person. It was the red one's.”)
>
> The red Gyarados in the Lake of Rage dreams of being small again. The creature on the lake lent her that dream, so she would know.
>
> She came back and asked me how long a Gyarados lives. I told her: longer than the people who made it. She did not answer.
