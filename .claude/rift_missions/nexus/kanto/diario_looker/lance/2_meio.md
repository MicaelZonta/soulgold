# Lance — página 2 (meio)

**Data no topo:** "tomorrow" (o Ancião escreve a data de amanhã na porta, na véspera) · **Tinta:** a letra firme, e a segunda, menor, na margem

> The date on the Elder's door is tomorrow's. He writes it the night before. He says it saves time.
>
> He showed me the clan's scroll of dragons. The first page is torn out. The edge is scorched.
>
> Past the pond, under the tower, is a valley on no map. Steam, ferns taller than houses, stone pillars broken at the height of a horn. The scorch marks match.
>
> The Champion does not fly up to train. He flies up to look for whoever tore that page out. He thinks the first dragon of his clan came from nowhere, and that the clan was ashamed of it.
>
> (In the margin, smaller: “Not ashamed. Afraid. What comes from nowhere can go back there.”)
>
> He asked me the Elder's question: what matters most to a Trainer. I said the truth. He said that was a detective's answer. See also File L-1020.
