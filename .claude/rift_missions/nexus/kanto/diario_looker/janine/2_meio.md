> Page two. Dated 'the same festival, the other end of it.' The ink smells of peaches.
>
> He is third in the fourth row. Smiling. Chewing slowly. He came here on a mission for a League in the south, to study a poison that makes loyalty.
>
> He found it. He tasted it, to be sure.
>
> She comes every morning and stands before him in his own clothes. He smiles at her the way he smiles at the lanterns.
>
> The big one with the chain broke its own statue open from the inside. The small one fills the bamboo with her father's face. They served the shell first.
>
> In a way, he serves it now too.
>
> She says she guards the three of them so no one else joins the rows.
>
> In the margin, a smaller hand: 'She is not guarding them. She is guarding him from them. Look again.'
