> September 31st.
>
> The theater is full now. Not with people: with costumes. Every disguise they ever wore, sitting upright in the seats. Hats on. Waiting.
>
> They have stopped looking. 'A motto is a promise,' the woman said. 'We just made it to the wrong person.'
>
> They stay by the stage door now. The Meowth tried to sell tickets. The song is free. He has made his peace with that. Mostly.
>
