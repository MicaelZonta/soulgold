# Agatha — página 1 (começo)

📝 **Proposta de 30/09/2026, aguardando o autor.** Caderno da campeã do Spectrier (File L-897, Shadow Steed) ([formato](../../../DIARIO_LOOKER.md)). Narração, sem plaquinha (R18).

**Data no topo:** “A Tuesday, forty years long.” · **Tinta:** molhada; letra firme, uma só.

> A Tuesday, forty years long.
>
> Kanto. In this one, no child has ever set out with a Pokédex. There is no Pokédex. Children stay home and help in the shops.
>
> So no one has ever beaten the Elite Four. They are still up on the plateau. Older every year. Undefeated every year.
>
> Lavender Town, at night. Black snow fell on the tower roof and did not melt. The townspeople swept it into the gutters and said nothing.
>
> Hooves on the roof. Round and round. On the tower steps, an old woman, a cane in one hand and a dark carrot in the other, holding it out to nothing.
>
> Something ate it. I did not see what. Her ghost laughed. She did not. 'It's looking for someone,' she said. 'Aren't we all.'
>
> See also: File L-897. Shadow Steed.
