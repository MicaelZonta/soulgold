> September 31st. Written once, right side up. The first straight thing in this notebook.
>
> She walked out to the lake today and looked at the bird kindly. It took all afternoon. Then the snow began to fall again.
>
> -- Other hand: Falling means time is moving. Good.
>
> The fold over the city is still there. The pearl-colored one sleeps in it. 'It came through a door I made,' she said. 'It may stay until it chooses.'
>
> She gave me a link of red chain she found in the crease of the horizon. It hums. -- Other hand: Wrong kind of hum. Keep it away from clocks.
>
> 'Someone will come looking for that,' she said. 'Someone who wants to hold the sky still.' Then she told me I had already left. I checked. I had.
