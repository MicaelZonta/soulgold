# Bruno — página 1 (começo)

**Data no topo:** "Day nine, I think." (não há sol para contar os dias) · **Tinta:** firme, uma letra só; molhada

> Day nine, I think. There is no sun here to count by.
>
> A jungle has grown over the road to the Indigo Plateau. Victory Road is under the roots. The League gates are under the leaves.
>
> Nobody has challenged the Elite Four in years. Nobody can reach them. The League keeps its doors open anyway, out of habit.
>
> Behind the second door a man is training. Push-ups, in the heat, in the dark. He counts out loud. He has reached a number I will not write down.
>
> He asked if I had come to challenge him. I said I was only a detective. He looked disappointed, and went back to counting.
>
> See also File UB-02.
