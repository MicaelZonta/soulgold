# Bruno — página 2 (meio)

**Data no topo:** "Day nine again." (o mesmo dia da página 1) · **Tinta:** a letra firme, e uma segunda, menor e mais nova, corrigindo na margem

> Day nine again. I am sure of it this time.
>
> A year ago the other three of the Elite Four went into the jungle to find what made it grow. They came back quiet. They sit down a great deal now.
>
> It drinks. Not blood, not water. Strength, from anything that stops. The trees stopped growing. The three of them stopped to rest.
>
> The man behind the second door has not stopped. Not once, in a year. He is not training. He is staying on his feet.
>
> (In the margin, in a smaller, neater hand: “Not awake. Moving. When he pauses, even for a breath, the vibrations in the jungle lean toward him.”)
>
> I did not write that. I will think about it later.
