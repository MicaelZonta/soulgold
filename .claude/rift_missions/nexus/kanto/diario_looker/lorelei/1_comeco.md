# Lorelei — página 1 (começo)

📝 **Proposta de 30/09/2026, aguardando o autor.** Caderno da campeã do Glastrier (File L-896, Wild Horse) ([formato](../../../DIARIO_LOOKER.md)). Narração, sem plaquinha (R18).

**Data no topo:** “The first day of winter.” (escrito três vezes) · **Tinta:** molhada; letra firme, uma só.

> The first day of winter. Written three times. None of them crossed out.
>
> Four Island, far to the south of Kanto. A small town, a daycare, and a cave of ice at the north end, where great sea creatures used to sing in the dark.
>
> They do not sing now. The islanders say men came with nets, years ago. A man in a long coat came after them, asked questions, and left on the next boat.
>
> Nobody from the League came home to help. In this Kanto, no one leaves the League. No one ever beat them, so no one was ever let go.
>
> Tonight one of them came home anyway. She resigned. The League did not know that was allowed.
>
> The island's only farm froze in one night. The wheat rang like bells. In the middle stood a white horse in a mask of ice, with a bell of ice around its neck.
>
> The tall woman in glasses stood in front of it with her arms out. She was not afraid of it. She was angry at it. Those are different things.
>
> See also: File L-896. Wild Horse.
