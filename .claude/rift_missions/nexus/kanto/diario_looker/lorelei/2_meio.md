# Lorelei — página 2 (meio)

📝 **Proposta de 30/09/2026, aguardando o autor.** Caderno da campeã do Glastrier (File L-896, Wild Horse) ([formato](../../../DIARIO_LOOKER.md)). Narração, sem plaquinha (R18).

**Data no topo:** “February 30th.” · **Tinta:** molhada; a mesma letra firme, e uma segunda, mais nova, corrigindo à margem.

> February 30th. The islanders use this date often. Nobody asks why.
>
> I was wrong about the horse. So was she.
>
> Three nights ago the boats came back. Nets, bright lights, men in black. They went into the cave for the singers.
>
> The horse got there first. One stamp, and the whole lake froze, top to bottom. The men could not break it. They left with empty nets and very cold boots.
>
> Under the ice, the singers are still singing. You can hear them if you lie flat on the shore. She did. So did I. The horse was already there, listening.
>
> As a girl, she said, she hid the same way the first time the boats came. Under her bed, very still, until it was over. 'It isn't cruel,' she said. 'It's keeping them.'
>
> In the margin, in another hand: 'Two vibrations under the ice. One is a song. The other is a heartbeat, waiting.'

**Fios:** **A mão que muda** (segunda letra na margem, fala de "vibrations"). A virada: o cavalo que “toma à força” (File L-896, fala do Pryce) aqui **protegeu** — congelou o lago para esconder os Lapras dos caçadores. É o que a variação 1 de campeão (“ice is cold because it's keeping something safe”) e a 3 (o lago congelado) contam sem contar. R21: no fragmento dela, a Lorelei era criança quando os barcos vieram pela primeira vez (variação 3 genérica). O “heartbeat, waiting” é a pedra da página 3.
