# Petrel — página 1 (começo)

**Data no topo:** "Opening night." (o cartaz diz que toda noite é noite de estreia) · **Tinta:** firme, uma letra só

> Opening night. The poster outside says every night is opening night.
>
> A theater in Goldenrod, and the whole audience is costumes. They sit up in the seats on their own, hats on, waiting. A song comes from an empty stage.
>
> The costumes belong to one man. Radio Director. Grunt. Tourist. Old woman. Policeman. And the boss of Team Rocket: suit, tie, a cat-shaped dent in the lap.
>
> He wears them in turns. The town never knows which one is him. On bad days, neither does he.
>
> I asked who the boss of Team Rocket really is. The whole theater laughed. Nobody here has ever seen him without the suit being empty first.
>
> See also File L-648.
