# Proton — página 2 (meio)

**Data no topo:** "Deeper than yesterday. Still dry." · **Tinta:** a letra firme, e a segunda, menor, na margem

> Deeper than yesterday. Still dry.
>
> He took the stake job. A valley south of the well, a cracked bowl in a shrine, stakes all around it. He pulled them one by one and sold each one.
>
> At the last stake the bowl woke. He expected a monster. It looked at him and did nothing. He calls it the worst insult of his career.
>
> (In the margin, smaller: “It did nothing because it was already full. His fear was in it. It had been for years.”)
>
> Then he did something nobody in town has seen. He went back to the well, sat by the last Slowpoke, and stayed to watch its tail grow back.
>
> It took eleven days. The Slowpoke noticed on the ninth. While he waited he found a pocket watch in the mud, stopped at a quarter past nine. Nobody would buy it.
