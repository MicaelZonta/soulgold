# Proton — página 1 (começo)

**Data no topo:** nenhuma (em Azalea escrevem a profundidade do poço no lugar da data: "Today: dry.") · **Tinta:** firme, uma letra só

> No date. In Azalea Town they have stopped writing dates. They write the depth of the well instead. Today: dry.
>
> The Slowpoke Well used to be full. The old story says a Slowpoke's yawn once brought rain and ended a drought here.
>
> In this Azalea, nobody stopped the tail-cutting. It went on for years. The Slowpoke stopped yawning. Then the rain stopped.
>
> The man in charge of the cutting still walks the town, grinning. People cross the street. He tips his hat to them.
>
> He sold tails to anyone. His best buyer was a laboratory on Cinnabar Island. His strangest wanted wooden stakes instead.
>
> See also File L-1003.
