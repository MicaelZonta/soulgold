# Archer — página 2 (meio)

**Data no topo:** "Day 1,096, again." (ele não mudou o número: "the day has not ended") · **Tinta:** a letra firme, e a segunda, menor, na margem

> Day 1,096 again. He did not change the number. He says the day has not ended.
>
> Behind the Tower there is a forest that was not there last week. Dead leaves, still falling. On every trunk, a wooden tablet, written edge to edge, and every line scratched out.
>
> I read what I could under the scratches. Dates. Orders. Sums paid for Slowpoke tails. A laboratory on Cinnabar Island, paid in full.
>
> It is his handwriting. The ledgers of Team Rocket, all of them. And the scratching is his handwriting too.
>
> On the last tablet, one line he did not finish scratching out. A reply to the broadcast, signed with one letter: “It is over. Go home. G.”
>
> (In the margin, smaller: “He scratched it out the same night it came. The grain of the wood remembers.”) See also File L-1001.
