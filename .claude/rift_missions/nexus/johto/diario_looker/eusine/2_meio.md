📝 Proposta de 30/09/2026, aguardando o autor. Caderno do campeão de Suicune, Raikou e Entei ([DIARIO_LOOKER.md](../../../DIARIO_LOOKER.md)). Narração, sem plaquinha.

> The hour of the lightning. Ten years and a day. Here is the turn. The watch is his. He stopped it, and he winds it backward every night.
>
> If the moment ends, the fire finishes. The three inside die. And then, the story goes, a bird of seven colors brings them back.
>
> But nobody here has seen that bird in a hundred and fifty years. His friend who sees the future will not say whether it comes.
>
> So he holds the moment. Nobody dies. Nobody is reborn. The north wind blows in place. The bolt hangs. The fire waits.
>
> This morning he found a feather and was overjoyed. It was silver. Wrong bird. He laughed until he had to sit down.
>
> In the margin, in a smaller hand: 'He is not holding the moment. It is holding him. The watch only vibrates when he lets go.'
