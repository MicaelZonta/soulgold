📝 Proposta de 30/09/2026, aguardando o autor. Caderno do campeão de Articuno e Glastrier ([DIARIO_LOOKER.md](../../../DIARIO_LOOKER.md)). Narração, sem plaquinha.

> Tuesday. I have stopped counting. He opened the drawer today, while I watched. Inside was a mask of ice that has not melted in fifty years.
>
> When he was young, he lost his first partner to the ice. On that pass. He never found it.
>
> He wore the mask when he meant to turn back time, by catching a small green traveler that lives in the forest to the west.
>
> He gathered children to help him, and gave them masks. Two of them I have met in other notebooks. One performs. One stays up late.
>
> Here is the turn. He never did it. One Tuesday he took off the mask, sent the children home, and walked up the pass instead.
>
> And it was Tuesday again. The day is waiting for him to put the mask back on, or throw it away.
>
> In the margin, in a smaller hand: 'Neither. He is waiting for a horse. Put your ear to the ground.'
