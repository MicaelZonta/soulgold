>
> The trees on the boulevard are in flower and in fruit at once. Nothing on them has ever dropped.
>
> The posters outside the theater are new. The film is old. The actress on the poster has not changed in fifty years.

> She received me in her dressing room. Warm. Gracious. Tired in a way the face does not show.
>
> On her table, a pocket watch someone left at the stage door. Stopped at a quarter past something. She keeps it because it is the only thing in Kalos that stops.
>
> “A detective!” she said. “How lovely. I played one once. He never solved anything.”
