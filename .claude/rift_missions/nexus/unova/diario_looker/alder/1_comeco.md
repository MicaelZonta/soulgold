> Not Unova. Ferns taller than houses, and air like soup. An old man by a fire waved me over as if he had been expecting me.

> He was Champion of Unova once. A pale young man with a black dragon beat him. An hour later, a girl came with a white one.

> He watched them both leave the League in different directions. He says it is the saddest thing he has seen, and he has seen a lot.

> Then he left too. Walked south, and then further. He says Unova has a new Champion now, and he likes her very much.
>
