## Texto

> He told me on the school roof. Once, he went to the Giant Chasm. Alone.
>
> Both of his friends were chosen by dragons. He went to find the third one, the one nobody took.
>
> It looked at him, and then it looked away. It was waiting for someone else. A woman with a cape, he thinks. He does not know how he knows.
>
> When he came back, men in grey uniforms were at the school. The children stood behind him. He had nothing ready.
>
> Something made of steel stepped between them. It did not attack. It stood there. The men left.
>
> In the margin, another hand: “He cried, after. That is not weakness.” He would want it corrected. I have not corrected it.
