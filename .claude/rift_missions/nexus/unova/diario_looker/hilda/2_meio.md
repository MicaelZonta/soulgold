## Texto

> I caught up with her at last. She was not running. She was sitting in the ash, and the dragon's tail was grey.

> “It does that when I lie,” she said. “I told my mom I was fine.” She showed me the reply. She had not sent it.

> The white one stays only with the truth. So she has told the truth for two years, to everyone. It has cost her friends.
