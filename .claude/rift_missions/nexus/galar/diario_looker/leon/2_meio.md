> The same night. The clocks say it is morning. The clocks are being polite.
>
> He told me the secret over a cup of tea gone cold. As a boy, he got lost in a foggy wood near his home. Of course he did.
>
> Two great wolves found him there. One carried a sword in its jaws. The other wore a shield. They looked at him for a long time.
>
> Then they turned and walked away into the fog. “They were looking for someone,” he said. “It wasn't me.” He laughed. His eyes did not.
>
> So on the darkest day, no sword came. No shield. Only a young man and his dragon, who refused to lose.
>
> He caught it himself. It took all night. It is still taking all night. The sky does not know it has lost.
>
> (In the margin, in another hand: “Two strong vibrations, very far apart. Neither of them is here.”)
