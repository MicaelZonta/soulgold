# Cyrus — página 2 (meio)

📝 **Proposta de 30/09/2026, aguardando o autor.** Caderno do campeão de Dialga (File L-483 Heartbeat) ([formato](../../../DIARIO_LOOKER.md)). Narração, sem plaquinha (R18).

**Data no topo:** “12:00. Still. (Written over twice.)” · **Tinta:** molhada; a mesma letra firme, e uma segunda, mais nova, corrigindo à margem.

> 12:00. Still.
>
> On his desk, a small pink creature in a glass case. It is the only other thing that moves. He took it from a lake so the world would stop feeling.
>
> It worked. For everyone but him. He says the feeling had to go somewhere, and he was the only one standing close enough.
>
> Every day, which is the same day, he walks to a house at the edge of the city. An old man sits at the table, halfway through a sentence to his grandson.
>
> The grandson stands there for hours. He has never let it finish. He does not know how it ends.
>
> One link of his red chain is new. Warmer than the rest. He cannot say where it came from. (In the margin, in another hand: “He is afraid. It is very loud.”)
