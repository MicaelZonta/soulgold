# Cynthia — página 2 (meio)

📝 **Proposta de 30/09/2026, aguardando o autor.** Caderno do campeão de Giratina (File L-487 Renegade, co-campeã com o Silver) ([formato](../../../DIARIO_LOOKER.md)). Narração, sem plaquinha (R18).

**Data no topo:** “The same spring. It has not moved.” · **Tinta:** molhada; a mesma letra firme, e uma segunda, mais nova, corrigindo à margem.

> The same spring. It has not moved.
>
> I found her. Or she found me. Here there is no down, and she walks on the underside of things as if she grew up there.
>
> At the summit, the man bound the lords of time and space with a chain of red crystal. She broke it with her bare hands.
>
> The links fell into the tear in the sky. So did she. Something with black wings caught her before she hit anything.
>
> In the dark she found a merchant's pack, very old. A ledger inside, in a hand very like her own. Its owner came here once to ask for power.
>
> She does not want its power. She wants to know its real name. (In the margin, in another hand: “It has not answered. It is listening.”)
