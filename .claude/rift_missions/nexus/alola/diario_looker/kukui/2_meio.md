# Kukui — página 2 (meio)

**Data no topo da página:** *The night that never came.* · **Tinta:** molhada · **Letra:** firme, com uma segunda letra na margem

> His wife studies the other side, the places past the holes in the sky. One evening she opened one to take a closer look.
>
> It closed behind her. Just like that. He says she was laughing when she went through. He tells it like a good story, and then he stops.
>
> That same night he found a small cloud on the beach, crying. He raised it in the lab. It grew, and grew, and began to glow.
>
> He asked it for one thing: keep the lights on, so she can find her way back. It did. It has not stopped since.
>
> Up on the mountain he built a stadium for a League. No one comes. Nobody here can sleep in this light, and the children are too tired to climb.
>
> Every night a guardian drops out of the storm to fight a man in a mask up there. The masked man loses. His elbow is bandaged in the same place as the professor's.
>
> (In the margin, a second hand: Same vibrations. Obviously.)
