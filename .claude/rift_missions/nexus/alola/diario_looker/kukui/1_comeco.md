# Kukui — página 1 (começo)

**Data no topo da página:** *12:00. (Which twelve?)* · **Tinta:** molhada, e seca rápido demais, como no sol · **Letra:** firme

> A beach town on a small island, and the brightest midnight I have ever seen. The clock tower says twelve. The sky says noon.
>
> Every shadow in the square points the same way, like people leaning away from a bonfire.
>
> A man in a lab coat and no shirt stood in the middle of it, taking notes, grinning. He said 'Alola, cousin!' before I could introduce myself.
>
> In the sand outside his lab I found a pocket watch, stopped at twelve. He looked at it for a long time. 'Noon or midnight?' he asked. Nobody here can tell anymore.
>
> On the table in his lab, two mugs. One is his. The other has dust in it.
>
