# Guzma — página 1 (começo)

**Data no topo da página:** *The day after the last day.* · **Tinta:** molhada; a página tem cheiro de chuva e de tinta spray · **Letra:** firme

> A city with half a street. The other half is not broken. It is missing, as if something took a bite and swallowed.
>
> It looks like an island town I have seen before. Same wall. Same rain. Only nobody is left to paint the wall.
>
> One young man is. White hair, black and gold, sunglasses he does not need in this dark. He was spraying a skull on a wall that ends halfway up.
>
> He said, 'You lost, old man? Yeah. Me too.' Then he went back to painting.
>
> Somewhere far off, something is still chewing. He counts the bites out loud. He is somewhere past nine hundred.
>
