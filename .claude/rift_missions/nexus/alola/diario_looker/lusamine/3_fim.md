# Lusamine — página 3 (fim)

**Data no topo da página:** *September 31st* · **Tinta:** molhada, e ainda morna · **Letra:** as duas se alternam; a troca é no meio da terceira caixa

> September 31st. Last night she opened one case. The first one, the pink one that used to sleep in her lap. It sneezed. The applause stopped.
>
> In the quiet, the staff began to blink, and then to leave, one by one, without saying goodbye. She let them.
>
> The line of spring crossed her steps this morning and went on up the stairs. The cases thawed behind it. The beads on the statue went -- dry. Cold. Glass again.
>
> She sits between the two doors now, the spring and the statue. She set the table for three. Two cups are still full. The tea is cold. She does not pour it out.
>
> She gave me a pocket watch that stopped at the hour her Paradise froze. 'I do not need to keep time anymore,' she said. 'Someone else might.'
