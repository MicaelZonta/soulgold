# Lusamine — página 2 (meio)

**Data no topo da página:** *The first day of spring. Written twice, crossed out once.* · **Tinta:** molhada · **Letra:** firme, com uma segunda letra na margem

> This morning a line crossed the snow, straight as a ruler. On one side, grass and warm wind. On the other, nothing moved.
>
> It came in from the sea, across the ice, and stopped at her front steps. Something up in the clouds was watching which side she would stand on.
>
> Her children left in the middle of winter, she told me. The girl with the bag. The boy whose partner wore a mask. She kept their rooms exactly as they were. Exactly.
>
> The applause began the day she hung the beads on her statue. A gift from a traveler with a sweet-smelling bird. She liked it. She liked it very much.
>
> In her mailbox this morning: a letter with no name, in a young man's hand. It says only, 'I'm still at the door.' It is not her son's writing. She read it four times.
>
> (In the margin, a second hand: Strong vibrations. She is deciding. Do not interrupt.)
