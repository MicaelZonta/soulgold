# Hau — página 2 (meio)

**Data no topo da página:** "A holiday. Every day here is a holiday." · **Tinta:** molhada. A letra do Looker, e uma segunda, menor e mais caprichada, corrigindo na margem.

> A holiday. Every day here is a holiday. Years ago, at this festival, the guardian of the island dropped a sparkling stone at his feet.
>
> In every version of the story I know, that stone falls for a stranger. Someone new to the islands. In his world, no stranger ever came.
>
> So it fell for him. Trials, Kahunas, the League. He won all of it. His grandfather was the last one he beat. He cried. The old man laughed so hard the stands shook.
>
> (In the margin, smaller and neater:) He wanted to be the one chasing. His vibration still leans forward, toward someone ahead of him.
>
> Nobody is ahead of him. He saves a malasada anyway. “For the new kid.”
>
> Today, on the empty seat beside his, a silver feather. He says it was not there yesterday. He let me take it.
