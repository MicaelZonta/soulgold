# Hau — página 1 (começo)

**Data no topo da página:** "The first day of the festival. Again." · **Tinta:** molhada. Letra firme, uma só (a do Looker).

> The first day of the festival. Again. Iki Town, Melemele Island.
>
> The lanterns have been hung so many times that the rope has worn grooves into the posts.
>
> Nobody is at the festival. The drums are there. The food stalls are there. The stage is there, scorched in rings.
>
> The boy is there. He is always there. He waved at me as if we had planned to meet.
>
> “Alola! You're not the new kid, huh? That's okay! Want a malasada?” I accepted. It was very good.
>
> He is the Champion of these islands. His name is on a wall in a stadium on the mountain. He says it gets lonely up there, so he comes down for the festival.
