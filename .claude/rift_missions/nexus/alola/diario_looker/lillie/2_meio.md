# Lillie — página 2 (meio)

**Data no topo da página:** *The next night, which was the same one.* · **Tinta:** molhada · **Letra:** firme, com uma segunda letra na margem

> The hole in the sky was hers, once. Small enough for a bag. She carried it until it could carry her.
>
> On its back she crossed to the other side and brought her mother home. Her brother followed a day later, with his partner, to help. The door closed before he could come back.
>
> She keeps his room. Not exactly as it was. She says that was her mother's mistake, and she is trying new ones.
>
> Her mother came home tired from the other side. On an island of flowers, a small bright guardian healed her. Then healed her again. And again. She slept among the flowers and did not wake.
>
> The girl went to the guardian and said, 'Thank you. That is enough.' It had never heard the word. Her mother opened her eyes that evening and asked for tea.
>
> (In the margin, a second hand: Brave. Recorded.)
