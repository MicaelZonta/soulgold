# Lillie — página 1 (começo)

**Data no topo da página:** *No moon. Night zero.* · **Tinta:** molhada, e as letras brilham de leve no escuro · **Letra:** firme

> A black lake in every direction, and stars resting on it without moving. There is no moon. There is a round hole where the moon should be.
>
> A girl in a white hat sat on the shore, writing in a notebook. She writes everything down, she says, so she won't get lost.
>
> She asked to borrow my pen. Hers had run out. We sat on the same shore writing in two notebooks. I did not read hers. She did not read mine.
>
> Beside her sat a partner with nine tails, white as frost, watching the hole in the sky as if it might fall.
>
> When the hole opened its wings, the stars went out, one row at a time. The girl waved. The hole waved back. I am told this is normal.
>
