# Gladion — página 1 (começo)

**Data no topo da página:** *Rain, day one. Written again underneath: Rain, day one.* · **Tinta:** molhada; escorreu com a chuva no canto de baixo · **Letra:** firme

> A walled town on a wet island. The gate hangs open. The sign on it has been painted over so many times it says nothing at all.
>
> Vines come over the top of the wall. Not slowly. I watched one reach the next brick while I tied my shoe.
>
> A young man keeps the gate. White hair, torn hood. His partner stands beside him with no mask at all, only the marks where one used to sit.
>
> I asked whom he was guarding the gate for. He said, 'The boss.' I asked where the boss was. He did not answer.
>
> A bell rang once, somewhere in the trees. He did not look up. I think he has stopped looking up.
>
