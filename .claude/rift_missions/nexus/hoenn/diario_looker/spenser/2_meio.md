📝 Proposta de 30/09/2026, aguardando o autor. Caderno do campeão de Celebi e Dialga ([DIARIO_LOOKER.md](../../../DIARIO_LOOKER.md)). Narração, sem plaquinha.

> Year: blank. Someone erased it. The eraser went through the paper.
>
> I understand the forest now. The trees are not different ages. They are different hours, and the hours do not belong here.
>
> Far away, a man at the top of a pillar of spears put a red chain on a great heart, and made it skip.
>
> The hours it skipped fell out of the world. They landed here, like leaves. The green light gathers them and plants them.
>
> Spenser has been the same age for forty years, he says. Skipped hours do not add up to a birthday.
>
> In the margin, another hand: “Not skipped. Stolen. The heart feels the gap. It beats harder to fill it.”
>
> I did not write that. The ink is still wet. See also File L-483.
