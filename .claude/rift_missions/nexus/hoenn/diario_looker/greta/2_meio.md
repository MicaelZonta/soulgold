> Date: noon again. The sun here does not move. I think something is holding it up.
>
> The shaggy ones fought each other every day. All Body, no judges. There were twelve. Now there is one, alone in a ring of broken tusks.
>
> The Tycoon's board: Body ten. Mind zero. Skill zero. She stopped writing after that. “Nobody should get that score,” she said.
>
> On the desk in the laboratory, a photograph. A woman with wild hair and a necklace of teeth. She walked through the door years ago. Not back.
>
> One creature did come back. Red, fast, always hungry. It sleeps by the door every night. It is waiting for her.
>
> In the margin, a second hand: “Not waiting. Guarding. The sun is its own. It keeps it lit so she can find the way.”
>
