> September 31st.
>
> The Tycoon made the red one a sandwich. It ate the plate. For a moment the sun dipped. Just a little. The first shadow on this island in years.
>
> She gave it a score. Body ten. Skill seven. Mind: “loyal,” which is not a number. She says the rules allow it now. She wrote the rules.
>
> A man in a long coat came through before me and asked about the door. She scored him too. Mind nine. Body, the other hand notes: “needs a nap.”
>
> The door has a twin. On a page I have not written yet, a man in a lab coat opens it the other way, into tomorrow. Both doors hum the same note.
>
> She guards this one now. Not to keep the old things in. To keep the red one company, until someone comes back through.
