**Topo da página:** “Day 1 of the voyage.” (Todas as linhas do diário de bordo dele dizem Day 1.) Tinta molhada; letra firme.

> Day 1 of the voyage. Every line in his logbook says Day 1.
>
> A ship has run aground on a plain of burned stone, far from any sea. Behind it, in the rock, there is still a wake.
>
> Above it, a storm that does not end. The lightning strikes the same spot again and again, a mile off the bow.
>
> The captain is an old sailor named Drake. This morning he hauled in his net, on dry land, and caught a single silver feather.
>
> He says it belongs to whatever keeps the sea. He says the sea sent it after him, to ask where he took the ship.
>
> Note. He has not asked me why I am here. Sailors do not ask. He only handed me a cup of something hot.
