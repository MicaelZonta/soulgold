> Year unknown. The stars moved a little last night. Not enough to count.
>
> It fell. The sky, I mean. A rock the size of a mountain, years ago. No one flew up to stop it. The island was called Mossdeep.
>
> The people went up in rockets from the Space Center. The last one never came down. He watched it until it was a star, and then until it was not.
>
> His secret. He was supposed to be at that launch. He stayed in the crater instead, sure the stones would tell him how to stop the next one.
>
> The steel shoots grew from the pieces he did not pick up. One of them has arms now, and they glow.
>
> A young woman in a long scarf came afterward and said she was sorry, she had been too late. She spoke of a sky that fell. He did not tell her it fell here too.
>
> I gave him the letter I have been carrying. It is from his father, who went up on the last rocket. He put it in his pocket, unopened, beside a stone that came to him from no one.
>
> In the margin, another hand: He sorts stones to keep his hands still. The vibration is in his hands.
