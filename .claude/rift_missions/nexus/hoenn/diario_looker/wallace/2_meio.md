> Date: the first of the same month. Again. I have stopped turning the calendar.
>
> Nobody in Sootopolis grows older. The old Gym Leader still dances every morning. He has been trying to retire for eleven years.
>
> Route 119. The fishermen still catch the plain brown fish, the one that becomes beautiful. They catch the same ones every day.
>
> None of them change anymore. Nothing here needs to. The Champion's own is the last one that ever did.
>
> The roots press on the crater walls, and the stone is turning to diamond. Last night something small and crowned walked out of it.
>
> It went straight toward the tree. He stood in its way, and bowed. It stopped.
>
> In the margin, a second hand, smaller: “Not a guard. An audience. He is waiting for something to end.”
>
