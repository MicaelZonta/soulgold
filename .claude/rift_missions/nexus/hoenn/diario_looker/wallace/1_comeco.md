> Date at the top: the first of a month with no name.
>
> Sootopolis, the crater city. Here, as in every version I have read, the two great ones of sea and land woke up and fought.
>
> Here, nobody stopped them. The rain drowned the east. The sun cracked the west. For one season, Hoenn was only mud and ash.
>
> Then a tree grew in the middle of the crater. Nobody planted it. Its branches shone in seven colors.
>
> Within a week everything was green again. Within a month everything was in bloom. It has not stopped since.
>
> The Gym Leader's pupil stands at the foot of the tree every morning. Cape, perfect posture. He bows to it. It does not bow back.
>
