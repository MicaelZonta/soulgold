**Data no topo da página:** "The day after the last day." · **Tinta:** molhada. A letra do Looker, e uma segunda, menor e mais caprichada, corrigindo na margem.

> The day after the last day. The Lorekeeper keeps one story, word for word. A rock the size of a mountain falls.
>
> A child comes from nowhere. The child rides the dragon of the sky up to meet it, and the rock is gone.
>
> In her world, the rock came. The child did not.
>
> She waited at the tower for seven days. On the eighth, she climbed up herself.
>
> It swallowed the rock, as the story says. The story says nothing about the dust.
>
> (In the margin, smaller and neater:) The rock was not empty. Something inside it was still moving. The vibrations stopped when it was swallowed.
>
> She does not mention that. Perhaps she does not know. Perhaps she knows. The Lorekeeper decides what the story says.
