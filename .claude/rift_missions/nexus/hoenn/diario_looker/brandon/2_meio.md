> Day three. Still.
>
> In the ice chamber there are footprints frozen in the floor, heading for the exit. They stop halfway.
>
> I asked Brandon whose they were. He laughed, and then he did not. “Mine,” he said. “Day three. I tried to leave.”
>
> The ice kept his footprints and let him walk back. He took that as an answer.
>
> The dots say the three golems are waiting for their maker. The one who towed the land with ropes, and went to sleep somewhere colder than here.
>
> Brandon has decided to wait with them. A king should keep his subjects company, he says. Even ones that are not his.
>
> In the margin, another hand: “The dots are newer than the dust. They were written after he arrived.” See also File L-378.
