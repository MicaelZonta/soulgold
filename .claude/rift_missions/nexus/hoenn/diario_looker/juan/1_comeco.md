>
> Sootopolis. The crater is full to the rim. The rooftops are under the water. The rain is warm.
>
> Two giants woke under this city, one of the sea and one of the land. Here, no one climbed the tower in the sky to stop them. The sea won.
>
> Every evening a man in a cape walks out onto the water and dances. There is no audience. He bows at the end anyway.
>
> His name is Juan. He was the Gym Leader. He says his best student left before the rain, to find a beauty that does not grow old.
>
> I asked where the people went. He pointed down, then up, then laughed. I did not understand any of the three.
>
> Note. The rain does not taste of the sea. It tastes of something older.
