> September 31st.
>
> The water has gone down a hand's width. It goes down a little every evening he dances without an audience.
>
> He does not dance for the lake now. He dances for the mist, and for the thing that runs. They are the only two who never left. He keeps their door.
>
> A silver feather drifted onto the water today. He gave it to the mist. The mist carried it off toward another sea. Vibration: west. Far. Salt.
>
> A young man in a purple cape came through, asking after a beast of the north wind that makes water clean. He left disappointed. He was looking in the wrong century.
>
> He asked me to tell his student he was right to leave. I did not ask which student. There are, I think, many of him.
