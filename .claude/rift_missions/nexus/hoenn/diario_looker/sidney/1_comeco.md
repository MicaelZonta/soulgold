> A town north of the long rainy route. Everything is grey stone. The people, the dogs, a boy with a kite. All of it mid-step.
>

>
> One man is not stone. Young, loud, grinning on a grey bench with a grey sandwich. His dog sleeps across his feet. His name is Sidney.
>
> He says a white Pokémon with a blade on its head came down from the mountain three days before. The town threw stones at it. It kept coming back.
>
> He went after it, to say sorry for the town. When he came back, the town was like this.
>
> Note. He grins when he talks about it. I do not think it is a grin.
