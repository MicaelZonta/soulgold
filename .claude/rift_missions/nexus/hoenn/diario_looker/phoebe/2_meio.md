**Topo da página:** “The festival, still. The candles here do not get any shorter.” Tinta molhada; letra firme, e uma segunda na margem.

> The festival, still. The candles here do not get any shorter.
>
> I went down with her. The graves stop after a while. After that there is only the laughing, and it is older than the graves.
>
> At the bottom, things fall up. Water, pebbles, my hat. Below the bottom there is another side, where everything is turned inside out. I did not look long.
>
> On the stairs she found a link of red chain, broken open. It was warm. She put it in her pocket and did not say why.
>
> Her secret. Her Dusclops stopped hearing the dead the night the summit fell. She has been laughing so the silence would not notice.
>
> In the margin, a second hand: She laughs on the breath out. Fear lives on the breath in.
