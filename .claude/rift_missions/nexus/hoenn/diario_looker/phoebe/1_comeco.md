**Topo da página:** “The festival of the dead.” (Uma noite que ninguém encerrou.) Tinta molhada; letra firme.

> The festival of the dead. It was supposed to last one night. No one ever ended it.
>
> Mt. Pyre. Except the mountain is gone. Where the summit was there is a hole, and the graves go down its walls like steps.
>
> Rust-colored plumes drift up out of it. There is no wind.
>
> A girl sits at the edge, swinging her legs over nothing. Phoebe. She trained here. Her grandmother kept two orbs at the top, red and blue.
>
> She says a man from the sea and a man from the land came for the orbs on the same night, and fought over them. The summit fell in, and the orbs with it.
>
> She has been going down to fetch them ever since. 'The ghosts are helping,' she says, and laughs.
>
