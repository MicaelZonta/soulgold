# Winona — página 3 (fim)

**Topo da página:** September 31st · **Tinta:** molhada; as duas letras se alternam no meio da terceira caixa. 📝 Proposta de 30/09/2026, aguardando o autor ([DIARIO_LOOKER.md](../../../DIARIO_LOOKER.md)).

> September 31st. The sky is still low. It will stay low. Winona has stopped measuring.
>
> She took down the bridge herself, knot by knot. Then she walked her birds out to the plain on foot, in a line. Altaria last, complaining.
>
> The golden runner came alongside them for a mile. Then it sprinted off, and every one of her birds went after it -- ran, not flew. Lighter vibration. They were laughing.
>
> The rider in the cloud has stopped throwing lightning at the roofs. There is nothing left up there to aim at. It seems relieved.
>
> On the last bridge post she found a feather. Silver, very long. Not from this sky. From higher. She gave it to the wind, and the wind took it toward the sea.
>
> She kept her door. Nobody else wanted it. Now it opens onto grass.
