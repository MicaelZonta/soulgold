# Winona — página 1 (começo)

**Topo da página:** "A Tuesday, twice." · **Tinta:** molhada; letra firme. 📝 Proposta de 30/09/2026, aguardando o autor ([DIARIO_LOOKER.md](../../../DIARIO_LOOKER.md)).

> Fortree. The treetop houses are where they should be. The sky is not. It hangs just above the highest roof, gray, and it is lower than yesterday.
>
> Winona measures it every morning with a knotted rope and a Swablu. Four knots lower this month.
>
> The birds of Fortree fly bent, heads down, under the cloud. Only one thing flies above it. It rides the cloud, laughs, and throws lightning at the roofs.
>
> Winona says the sky began to sink on the night a green streak went up past the clouds and did not come back.
>
> She has built a rope bridge from the tallest house up into the cloud. She walks it every evening. She says she is keeping it open.
>
