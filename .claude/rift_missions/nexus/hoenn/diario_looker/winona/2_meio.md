# Winona — página 2 (meio)

**Topo da página:** "The Tuesday again." · **Tinta:** molhada; na margem, a segunda letra. 📝 Proposta de 30/09/2026, aguardando o autor ([DIARIO_LOOKER.md](../../../DIARIO_LOOKER.md)).

> Top of the page: the Tuesday again. The rope says the sky has not moved. First time in a month.
>
> Walked Winona's bridge into the cloud. Inside, it is loud. The rider is there, laughing, throwing lightning. Not down. Up.
>
> Every bolt pushes the cloud a little higher. The ones that hit the roofs are the ones that miss.
>
> (In the margin, a smaller hand: It is not laughing. It is straining. Listen to the vibration.)
>
> Came down on the far side of the cloud. A plain. Grooves in the dirt. Something golden running, wings folded, faster than the storm.
>
> Winona saw it too. She sat down in the grass for a long time. Then she said, quietly: they don't need the sky.
>
> She did not say who. I think she meant her birds. I think she meant herself.
