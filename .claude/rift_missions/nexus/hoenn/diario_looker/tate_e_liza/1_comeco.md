> Mossdeep is two islands now. A clean cut runs through the middle, from the Space Center to the shore. The sea fills it. Both faces of the cut are smooth as mirrors.
>
> On one half, the Gym, and Tate. On the other, the Space Center, and Liza. They have not stood in the same room for a year.
>
> They talk anyway. At dusk, pictures cross the channel through the air: what he saw today, what she ate. Something blue carries them. Something red listens in, invisible, and laughs.
>
> Tate says the cut happened in one night, with a sound like a bell. In the morning a horned thing of stone and metal stood in the channel, humming.
>
> Liza says it was not one night. She says she remembers the cut from before it happened.
