> September 31st. The channel is still there. They have built a bridge over it. It is very narrow. Only one person can cross at a time.
>
> Last night the blue one fell. Too many pictures, too many years. It dropped into the channel like a stone.
>
> Tate jumped from his side. Liza jumped from hers. They did not look at each other first. They met in the middle and held it up between them until it could fly.
>
> The horned thing watched from the shallows. Then it walked out to sea, humming, and did not cut anything else.
>
> Where the blue one fell, they found a small stone on the bottom with a strand of color inside. A Mega Stone. No owner. The red one would not -- touch it. Too heavy a vibration for someone so light.
>
> They gave it to the man from the house on the hill, the one who collects stones. He left on the first boat. He always does.
