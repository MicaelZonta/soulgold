> Date: Tuesday. The same Tuesday, I am nearly certain.
>
> She opened the fourth door. She says she did not decide to. Her hand did, the way it does at the Pike.
>
> Behind it: a sky with no stars, and no floor. Three lights coming. The middle one flying blind, trusting the other two.
>
> The green lights left every leaf on the island at once and ran together to the door. They made a long shape. Coiled. Black and green.
>
> She stood next to it. Nobody asked her to. The three lights stopped a meter from her face, listened, and turned back.
>
> From the beach, afterwards, I looked at the building. It was never shaped like a Seviper. It was shaped like the thing at the door.
>
> In the margin, a second hand: “The vibrations match. The building was a warning. Somebody built it knowing.”
>
