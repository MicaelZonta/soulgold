> September 31st.
>
> The Beast Ball has a crack in it. Hairline. She measures it every morning. Less than a millimeter a day.
>
> The creature from the crater waits outside her door. It has not attacked. It changes shape each time she opens it, as if trying out an answer.
>
> She has decided she is the door between them. One wants out. One wants in. She sleeps sitting up.
>
> This morning the ball on her belt was a different ball. No crack. The cracked one is gone. She says she did not drop it.
>
> A man in a long coat had asked her about it the week before. She answered every question precisely. He did not write any of it down.
>
> Under my last line, for the first time, the smaller hand: “I've checked that memory twice. It holds.”
