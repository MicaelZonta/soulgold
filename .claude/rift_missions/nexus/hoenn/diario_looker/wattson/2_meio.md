> Top of the page: 4:47. Again. I have written three days today.
>
> Found what the clocks are for. Under the Gym, under the workshop, there is a second city. New Mauville. Its generator has been about to explode for one minute. For years.
>
> Wattson could not fix it. So he stopped the minute. Every clock in the city holds it still.
>
> Something helps him. A small figure of pink and steel sits in the generator room with its chest open, drinking the extra current. Five hundred years old. It curtsied at me.
>
> And something runs the wires all night, carrying the rest away, too fast for the lamps. I saw it once. Claws. A grin.
>
> (In the margin, a smaller hand: Not a grin. It was out of breath.)
>
> He is not guarding a door. He is guarding a minute.
