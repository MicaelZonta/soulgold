> Top of the page: the hottest day of the year. The margin says: again.
>
> Inside the door, a mountain lit from below. Something walks the ceiling on cross-shaped feet. Under it, set in the rock, a red stone like a heart. The creature never looks away from it.
>
> That stone keeps the mountain's heat inside the mountain. Take it, and Lavaridge cooks. Someone tried. The claw marks on the ceiling say the creature objected.
>
> Deeper, a lake of melted rock, and a small thing swimming in circles. On the shore, jade beads. Standing near them, I felt smaller.
>
> Flannery would not go near the beads. They were her grandfather's. She wore them for a year after he went up the mountain, to feel as strong as he was.
>
> She says the wanting went into the beads. One morning they were gone from her neck, and something was swimming in the lake.
>
> (In the margin, a smaller hand: She did not lose them. She threw them. Different vibration.)
