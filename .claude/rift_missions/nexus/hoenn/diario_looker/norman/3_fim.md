> September 31st. The sun came up. Partly.
>
> Last night the great wolf stood up from among the shields and walked to Norman. They stood side by side. Neither of them faced the dark. Both of them faced the child.
>
> At dawn the child walked out of the jungle alone, looked at them both, and kept walking. Down the road. Away.
>
> Norman did not follow. He raised one hand. At the edge of the trees, the one with crossed arms raised one too.
>
> Beside the largest shield there is a hole in the shape of a blade. Weeks ago, Norman says, a cocky young man with spiky hair pulled something out of it and -- walked off toward the dark. Wrong hands. The blade hummed wrong.
>
> The dark is thinner here now. Not gone. Someone else was supposed to stop it.
