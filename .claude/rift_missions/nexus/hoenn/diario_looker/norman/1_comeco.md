> Petalburg. The sky has been dark for eleven days. The Gym is the only building with its lights on.
>
> Norman stands at the Gym door. He has not sat down in eleven days. His wife brings him tea. He drinks it standing.
>
> Past the edge of town, a field that is on no map. Shields planted in the mud, all facing the woods. Nobody planted them. They were there when the dark came.
>
> The woods have grown. Petalburg Woods is a jungle now. Vines reach toward the town and pull back, as if told not to.
>
> His child left on a journey before the dark came. He does not know where. He has not closed the door since.
